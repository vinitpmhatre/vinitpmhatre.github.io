Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for clinics evaluating an AI front desk.

Is ClinicVoice a replacement for clinic staff?

No. It is designed to automate routine administrative communication and surface requests that need staff attention.

Can ClinicVoice give medical advice?

The intended MVP workflow is not to diagnose or prescribe. Clinical or uncertain requests should be escalated according to the clinic's workflow.

Can a clinic customize what the AI says?

Yes. The product is being designed around clinic-specific hours, services, FAQs, appointment rules and escalation instructions.

Can I see the product before signing up?

Yes. Use the demo dashboard to explore the current MVP experience, or book a demo to discuss a clinic-specific workflow.
